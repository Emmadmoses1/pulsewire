About WAVZO

WAVZO is a Nigerian music, news and culture website. We bring you new songs, gospel and Afrobeats, breaking news, sports, entertainment and trending stories in one fast, easy-to-read place.

What you will find here

  • Music and gospel — new releases, lyrics, artist profiles and song pages.
  • News and sports — headlines from Nigeria and around the world.
  • Entertainment, culture and trending — what people are talking about right now.

How we work

  • When we cover a story that first appeared elsewhere, we link to and credit the original source at the end of the post.
  • If you spot a mistake, tell us and we will correct it.
  • Artists and labels are welcome to send music, press releases and news for consideration.

Get in touch

For news tips, song submissions, advertising or corrections, visit our Contact page. Copyright owners can find our takedown process on the DMCA page.
